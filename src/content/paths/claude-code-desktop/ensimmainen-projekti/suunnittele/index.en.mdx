Before building, you plan. In this step you decide what goes on the site, and Claude turns it into a plan.

## 1. Choose Plan mode

Below the text box, choose **Plan** as the mode. (You can also open the mode menu by pressing Ctrl+Shift+M on Windows or Cmd+Shift+M on a Mac.) In <Term id="plan-mode">Plan mode</Term>, Claude investigates and plans, but doesn’t change files.

<NuppuSays pose="tarkistaa">First write down for yourself what goes on the site. After that, writing the prompt is easy.</NuppuSays>

## 2. Say what you want

Write a prompt that says what, why and within which limits. An example:

```
I want to make a simple website that presents my dog Buttons.
The page has a heading, a short introduction, three fun facts and a place for a picture.
The readers are my friends and relatives, so the tone can be warm and playful.

Limits:
- Keep the site simple: it must open directly in the browser, and nothing should need to be installed.
- The site must also work well on a phone.

Make a plan first. If anything is unclear, ask me.
```

Claude may first ask some clarifying questions. It shows the options and marks the one it suggests as *Recommended*. Choose a suitable option, and Claude moves on to the plan.

## 3. Read and adjust the plan

When the plan is ready, a card **Claude proposed a plan** appears in the conversation. Click **Open plan**, and the plan opens in a panel next to the conversation. In it, Claude tells you which files it will make and what goes in them. Read the plan calmly.

<Screenshot id="r01-plan" title="plan card" alt="A conversation in Plan mode. Claude has asked two clarifying questions, and the chosen answers are marked as recommended. At the bottom is the card Claude proposed a plan, with the link Open plan and the buttons Reject, Revise…, Accept and the highlighted Accept and use auto mode." />

Ask for changes in your own words, for example “Add contact details at the end” or “The colors could be shades of green”: click the card’s **Revise…** button, type your wishes in the box and click **Revise**. You can also select text in the panel and comment on it.

Finally, ask it to add the project’s sticky note to the plan:

```
Also add a CLAUDE.md to the plan that records the project's goal and the limits above.
```

Don’t approve the plan yet. When you’re happy with it, move on to the next topic, where you start building.
