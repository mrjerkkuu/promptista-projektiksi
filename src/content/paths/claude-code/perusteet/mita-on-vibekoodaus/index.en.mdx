<Term id="vibekoodaus">Vibe coding</Term> is programming where AI writes the code and you steer. You say in your own words what you want, for example “make a page with a picture of my dog and a list of its tricks”. The AI writes the code, you look at the result and say what should change. Then the round starts again.

This guide uses <Term id="claude-code">Claude Code</Term>. It’s an AI assistant made by Anthropic that can work directly on your own computer.

## What is it good for?

- Small websites: a profile page, a recipe collection, a party invitation.
- Tools of your own: a calculator, a to-do list, a small game.
- Experiments: you quickly find out whether an idea could work.

## What isn’t it good at (yet)?

- AI can be wrong even when it sounds confident. Always check the result yourself.
- Large and complex systems still require a lot of expertise.
- If an app handles other people’s data or money, you need an expert’s help to make sure it’s secure.

<NuppuSays pose="iloitsee">Good news: you don’t need to know code by heart. The most important thing is to say clearly what you want, and we’ll practice that together.</NuppuSays>

## Your role

You’re the client and the quality inspector. The most important skill isn’t writing code but explaining clearly: what you want, why and within which limits. You’ll practice that a lot in this guide.

<ReadMore title="Where does the name come from?">

AI researcher Andrej Karpathy introduced the phrase *vibe coding* in February 2025. He used it to describe a way of coding where you let the AI take the lead and hardly read the code. Today the word is used more broadly for any programming done with the help of AI.

This guide emphasizes that you stay at the controls: you plan, check and save your work.
