This is an optional extra task. You publish your site with <Term id="github-pages">GitHub Pages</Term> at its own address that you can send to anyone. The service is free for public repos.

## 1. Turn on GitHub Pages

1. Open your repo on GitHub (the command `gh browse`).
2. Choose **Settings** in the top menu and **Pages** on the left.
3. Under **Build and deployment**, choose **Deploy from a branch** in the **Source** menu.
4. Choose **main** as the branch and **/ (root)** as the folder. Press **Save**.

Source: [GitHub Pages publishing source settings](https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/configuring-a-publishing-source-for-your-github-pages-site).

<NuppuSays pose="julkaisee">Three, two, one… launch! Once the site is on the web, you can send the link to anyone.</NuppuSays>

## 2. Wait a moment and open the site

Publishing usually takes a few minutes. Your site’s address looks like this:

```
https://your-username.github.io/my-site/
```

The address also appears on the same Pages settings page once publishing is done.

## 3. Update the site

Every `git push` updates the published site automatically within a few minutes.

<Tip type="warning" title="Everyone can see the site">

According to GitHub’s documentation, Pages sites are publicly visible on the internet. Don’t publish personal information you don’t want everyone to see, such as your address or phone number.

</Tip>

If the site doesn’t show up, see [troubleshooting](#/vianmaaritys/pages-ei-nay).
